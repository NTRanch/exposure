## What the source is, in one paragraph

A 14.8 s two-shot excerpt of a deep-throating performance: wide
overhead framing, one cut at 49% to a held face close-up, a
0.67–0.93 s stroke cycle with ±30% humanised spacing (≈ 17–19
cycles), depth effort peaking ≈ 70% through, no climax and no
conclusion — the take stops mid-cycle. The headline technique:
**the face is phase-locked to the stroke** — gaze up into the
camera at the top of every cycle, eyes closed at the bottom,
cheek fullness scaling with depth.

## What carries over

- **The cycle as data.** Period nominal 24 steps of the core's
  fixed step (the recipe's band, 20–28), per-cycle jitter ±30%
  from a seeded, authored table — the core stays deterministic;
  the humanisation is data. Depth curve: descent ≈ 45% of cycle,
  bottom dwell ≈ 15%, withdrawal ≈ 40%.
- **The phase-locked face**, translated into the draw-list idiom
  (below): eyes that open to the house at the top of the cycle
  and close at the bottom are *geometry* here — line and circle
  ops — which suits a live stage: the house reads her face at
  distance, in silhouette-and-shape language.
- **The effort envelope.** Depth effort peaks ≈ 70% through the
  routine and the routine ends mid-cycle, unwitnessed gains still
  pending — the source's non-ending becomes a scheduling fact
  (below), not a missing climax.

## What changes for the style

Exposure has no camera and no cut: it has a stage, a day, and a
ledger. The source's two-shot grammar is discarded entirely —
its *edit* is Redlight's inheritance, not this game's. What this
game takes is the performance itself, restaged as a live routine
inside the day's schedule: a schedulable slot activity ("live
appearance" context family) in which the act is performed as
draw-list choreography and paid through the attention/appraisal
economy. Where Redlight's adaptation is about being *recorded*,
this one is about being *seen, live, and only paid when
witnessed* — exposure the mechanic paying exposure the fantasy,
in the port-back's own terms.

## The draw-list performance (960×960, palette indices, r/c/l/p/t ops)

- **Figures as masses.** Two figure masses built from `c` (circle)
  and `r` (rect) ops in silhouette palette indices against the
  stage's dark index: the partner as a fixed anchor mass at stage
  bottom (the source's planted-hands law becomes *the anchor does
  not move* — all motion is hers), Lux as the moving mass.
- **The stroke as geometry.** Her head mass rides the cycle's
  depth curve along one authored axis: head `c` op centre
  interpolating between the shallow and deep anchor points with
  the 45/15/40 phase split; torso mass counter-shifts at half
  amplitude. No rotation channel exists in the idiom — head tilt
  is expressed as the face cluster's offset, not a transform.
- **Face cluster (the phase lock, drawn).** A small op cluster at
  the head mass: eye `l` ops that open (long line, iris `c` in
  the accent index, emissive-iris read as palette choice) at the
  top-of-cycle GAZE state and narrow to slits/closed (short or
  absent line) at the DEEP state; cheek `c` radius ∝ current
  depth, peaking in the dwell. States interpolate through the
  cycle phase — the cluster is a function of phase, never a
  swapped sprite. These are the estate face-state machinery's
  two phase-keyed states (GAZE / DEEP) rendered in this game's
  shape language; no peak/culmination state is used — the
  source has none.
- **Secondary motion.** Hair mass: one trailing `c` cluster
  following the head centre with a one-step lag and 0.6 amplitude
  factor — a *designed* lag in the deterministic idiom (the
  source's own hair lag was below measurement floor; this is
  the idiom's convention, not a claim about the source).

## Mechanics mapping (onto Exposure's existing systems)

- **A new context, ledger-paid.** The routine is a context in
  the exposure ledger's sense: first performance in a distinct
  context pays in full; repetitions pay ¼, counted per day, per
  the ledger rule. It slots into the day like the shoot or the
  recorded set — the player schedules it, it is not a cutscene.
- **Gains stay PENDING until appraisal.** The routine earns in
  blocks: one block per 6 completed cycles (the source's ≈ 18
  cycles = 3 blocks per full routine), each block's gain shown
  as gold "+N PENDING — UNWITNESSED" from the moment it is
  earned, joining her tracks only at the day's appraisal, per
  the pending-until-witnessed UI law. Gains route to the
  exhibitionism and recorded/voyeurism tracks among her four.
- **Cap and drift unchanged.** Track cap 140, thresholds
  Stirring 40 / Forming 100, overflow drifting off at appraisal
  — all as shipped. A full routine's 3 blocks are sized so a
  routine alone cannot tip a track; tipping stays a witnessed,
  multi-context event (the Stirring → Forming tip, if the
  routine's pending gains complete it, happens *at appraisal*,
  in front of the player, per the validated design).
- **Attention during the routine.** The Lens Toy programme
  model applies as shipped: attention decays ×0.88 per slot
  between beats. Inside the routine, the GAZE state is the
  praise surface: praise events landing while her drawn eyes
  are open to the house earn the ×3/2 weighting **while a
  recorded context is warm (≤ 2 slots)** — so the day's
  scheduling puzzle is real: run the recorded set within two
  slots before the live routine and her gaze beats pay warm;
  run it cold and they pay base. The routine thus couples the
  day's recorded and live contexts exactly the way the shipped
  economy rewards.
- **The written record closes it.** The day's record names the
  routine among the day's contexts with its blocks earned and
  witnessed, in the existing closing shape.
