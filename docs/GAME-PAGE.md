## What you do

Each day has six slots, and every slot offers three engagements. Choose one with the arrow keys and book it with Enter. Its gains go pending — nothing about her moves until the appraisal witnesses the day, once after slot three and again at the close. That is when tracks tip from Stirring into Forming.

The ledger logs contexts, not repetitions: the first shoot of a kind counts in full, and repeats count at a quarter. Attention is the day's economy — it decays between slots, and praise programmes earn half again while a recorded context, such as a set, a feed, or a tape, is still warm. Across three days the targets rise: form one track and hold 300 attention, then form two with 450 attention across six contexts, then form three with 600 attention across six contexts.

## Features

- Three days — The Shoot, The Appearance, and The Set — with rising formation, attention, and context targets.
- An appraisal system: gains stay pending until the day is witnessed, after slot three and again at the close.
- Settings for pace and text size, and the written record — days closed and best formation — persist in the browser, with settings reachable before any play.
